About AI Proof

Verifiable AI training data. Permanent. Immutable. Trustless.

The Problem

AI companies are being sued for training on copyrighted data. Researchers can't prove when their datasets existed. Regulators can't verify training data provenance.

⚠️ The crisis: No one can prove what data trained an AI model.

The Solution

AI Proof permanently stores dataset proofs on the Sui blockchain via Walrus decentralized storage. Anyone can verify when a dataset existed and what it contained — no trusted third party required.

✅ Cryptographically verified. Immutable. Permanent.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Walrus?

Walrus is a decentralized storage protocol built on the Sui blockchain. Your data is stored permanently across multiple nodes — no single company controls your files.

Why do I need to prove my dataset existed?

AI companies are being sued for training on copyrighted data. Proving when your dataset existed helps establish prior art, ownership, and regulatory compliance.

Is this free?

Yes. This is a hackathon project demonstrating Walrus + Tatum integration. Upload and verification are completely free.

Can I delete my dataset?

No. Once data is stored on Walrus, it's permanent. That's the point — immutable proof that cannot be altered or deleted.

What file types are supported?

Any file type — text, CSV, JSON, images, PDFs. Walrus stores raw blobs of data up to 50 MiB.
